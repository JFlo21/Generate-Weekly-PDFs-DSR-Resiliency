<objective>
Close the source-3 half of gap G-12-3: `scripts/backfill_claim_time_attribution.py`
extracts a claimer from `public.artifacts.filename` but strips only a `_<6hex>.xlsx`
hash tail. Production filenames carry no hash, so the `.xlsx` extension survives on the
extracted candidate, `is_sentinel_claimer("Unknown_Foreman.xlsx")` returns False because
the extension defeats its normalization, and 4,070 of 4,762 live proposals became the
literal placeholder string.

Purpose: this is the defect Juan's REJECT verdict named. Until it is fixed, an `--apply`
would freeze a placeholder as a permanent "real" name across 69 WRs — the exact outcome
D-12-A's provenance columns exist to make auditable and the exact outcome OWN-03 exists
to prevent.

Output: a surgical fix to `_extract_claimer_from_filename`, a defensive proposed-value
guard in `_build_apply_payload`, and source-3 fixtures rebuilt from the filename shape
`scripts/publish_artifacts_to_supabase.py::_parse_stable` actually writes.
</objective>

<objective>
Execute the OWN-03 remediation against production: dry-run, owner review, the one-way live
apply, and the post-run verification that the 93 affected WRs now regenerate under real
names.

Purpose: every earlier plan in Phase 12 is code and documentation with zero production
effect. This plan is where the 5,824+ rows frozen as `Unknown Foreman` are actually
repaired. Every task here is a human checkpoint by construction — Supabase writes to
billing attribution, and the Smartsheet attachment churn that follows, are exactly the
protected surfaces `~/.claude/rules/production-guardrails.md` reserves for Juan.

Decisions this plan exercises: **D-12-A** — the repair lands in
`billing_audit.attribution_snapshot` with `backfill_source` / `backfill_run_id` provenance,
not in a new table, so the proof of success is the NEXT scheduled run's behavior rather
than a new query surface. **D-12-B** — the known-good sample resolves through
`backfill_hash_history`, so a source-4 miss on WR 19073866 is a signal that the Supabase
hash store read is wrong, not that the sample is wrong.

Output: a recorded dry-run review, a recorded apply decision, the apply tallies, and the
post-run observation — all in `12-06-SUMMARY.md`.
</objective>

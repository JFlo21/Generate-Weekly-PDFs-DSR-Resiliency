<objective>
Close the server-side half of gap G-12-3. The STEP 4 RPC already validates the PROPOSED
value with `IF billing_audit.is_sentinel_value(v_row.value) THEN RAISE EXCEPTION`, but
`is_sentinel_value('Unknown Foreman.xlsx')` returns FALSE: its normalization strips
whitespace and maps `_` to a space, and nothing removes the trailing extension, so the
placeholder slips past a guard that was written specifically to stop it. The same blindness
is what let the Python side propose it in the first place.

Purpose: 12-07 fixes the parser that produced the bad value, but defense in depth is the
whole point of having a server-side guard at all. Any future source — cell history, an
operator CSV, a hand-built payload — could carry a filename fragment. The database is the
last place that can refuse it.

Output: one additional validation-loop check in `billing_audit/own03_backfill_attribution.sql`,
a contract test pinning its extension list to the Python constant, an updated RPC contract
in `billing_audit/schema.sql`, and Juan's hands-on re-apply of STEP 4 + STEP 5 in production.
</objective>

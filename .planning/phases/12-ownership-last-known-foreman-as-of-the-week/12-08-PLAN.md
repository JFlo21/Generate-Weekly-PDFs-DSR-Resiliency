unambiguous re-run scope and Phase 12 has a closable success criterion.

normalizes any `#`-prefixed value to NULL, so those rows arrive at the backfill script as
blank and are invisible to its named-sentinel targeting. They were silently outside the
5,829-row scope of the rejected dry-run.

as written cannot be satisfied from Supabase as populated, so the phase cannot close
against it.

what production data gets rewritten (question 1) or what "done" means (question 2), which
`~/.claude/rules/production-guardrails.md` reserves for Juan.

scope statement in the runbook, and one dated Living Ledger entry.

  sentinel as no-history.

  flag that widens targeting from named sentinels to anything `is_sentinel_claimer` accepts,
  including the NULL a `#NO MATCH` row arrives as — but it cannot distinguish a `#NO MATCH`
  row from a role that was never populated.

  its `group_content_hash` identifiers are sentinel-only, so it exercises source 3
  (`backfill_artifacts`), not source 4 (`backfill_hash_history`). The retired
  `hash_history.json` is not on disk.

<objective>
Extend the three lifecycle consumers that decide what happens to a file AFTER it is generated: the
cleanup orphan-supersede gate, the artifact publisher's variant parser, and the portal's label map.

Purpose: two of these were not in the phase's original audit anchor list and were found only by
reading the full modules. Both fail silently — a missed cleanup gate leaves a stale primary
attachment forever, and a missed publisher branch files a Helper #2 artifact under the wrong variant
with nothing but a warning. Neither surfaces in a check that only asks whether a file was produced.

Output: `helper2` variants recognized by the cleanup orphan gate, parsed with correct precedence by
the publisher, labelled in the portal, and pinned by an extended family-parity invariant.
</objective>

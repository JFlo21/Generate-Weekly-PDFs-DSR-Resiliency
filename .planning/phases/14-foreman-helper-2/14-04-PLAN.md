<objective>
Give run memory a Helper #2 shape: four additive nullable `row_state` columns, the matching writer
payload fields, and an explicit, recorded decision on whether those fields join the content hash.

Purpose: D-14-08 hands this choice to the planner with the instruction that the cost be stated, not
buried. The decision taken here is INCLUDE — the Helper #2 fields join `HASH_FIELDS` now, while
`RUN_MEMORY_INCREMENTAL_ENABLED` is off in production and the one-time churn is a shadow-mode cost
only. Deferring means paying the identical churn later, at a moment when incremental is live and the
same churn could trigger a regeneration storm.

Output: the additive DDL behind an owner checkpoint, the writer payload, the hash decision with its
cost written down, and the mirrored field list in the passive-compare script updated in the same
change so the two enumerations cannot drift.
</objective>

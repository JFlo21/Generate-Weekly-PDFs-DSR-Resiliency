Expand the proven tracer sideways to the subcontractor case: a Helper #2 completion on a
subcontractor row must produce the two shadow artifacts that pay the subcontractor, route to both
sheets, render the right header, parse back correctly, and hash correctly when several Helper #2
foremen share one aggregation bucket.

guard applied to a primary-key branch and forgotten on its sibling helper-key branch, producing a
live duplicate-billing artifact. The other was an exact-match variant gate that let a new variant
fall through to the wrong display path. Both are reproduced exactly if this expansion is written from
scratch instead of copied from the adjacent Helper #1 site.

Output: the reduced-sub and AEP-billable Helper #2 shadow variants, wired through grouping, workbook
rendering, filename parsing, aggregated hashing, and upload routing.

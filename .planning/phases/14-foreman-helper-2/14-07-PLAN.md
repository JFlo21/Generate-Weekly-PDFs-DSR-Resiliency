<objective>
Close the one suppression mechanism 14-RESEARCH.md confirmed has no self-correcting behavior: a
`sheet_registry` row whose stored column mapping was written before the Helper #2 synonyms existed is
admitted from cache on every unchanged run, so Helper #2 would silently never activate on that sheet
until its version happened to change for an unrelated reason.

Purpose: this is not a performance detail. It is the difference between "Helper #2 shipped" and
"Helper #2 shipped and quietly does nothing on the sheets that already existed." The fix is one
bounded revalidation: a mapping-schema marker on the registry row, checked as a sixth admission
condition, so any doubt still means full validation.

Output: the marker column behind an owner checkpoint, the sixth admission condition with a
not-yet-migrated degrade, the four distinguishable capability reasons, and the Intake-8-shaped
fixture that satisfies HLP-04 without touching a live sheet.
</objective>

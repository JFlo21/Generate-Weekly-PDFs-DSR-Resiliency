pre-seeding the existing live-attachment memo from two bulk sheet-level
listings, instead of paying one serial `list_row_attachments` call per
skip-candidate row inside the group loop.

live existence confirmation issues a per-row Smartsheet call from inside the
serial loop. The existence semantics are correct and must stay — only the
call pattern changes: 2 bulk calls up front instead of ~1,900 serial ones.

`_preseed_live_attachment_listings` helper with a `total_count` pre-flight and
a size ceiling, a pre-seed block wired in immediately after the memo is
declared, new tests, and a dated Living Ledger entry — with
`_live_row_attachments` and both of its call sites untouched.

watermarks by ~14:28Z, and at 15:19Z every sheet's live version had moved again. The four
observations line up on wall-clock hours, not on code: 117/121 at 19:56 and 22:20 CDT, 0/121
at 08:17 and 10:19 CDT. The registry write/compare path is unchanged since the 117/121 builds
(`git log 3f81d94..511ec48` touches only `orchestrate.py` at lines ~231/270/408/3119/3521, far

yet all 121 versions moved). Unverified; a two-point `get_sheet_version` sample on an old backup
sheet during business hours (read-only, Smartsheet MCP) would confirm it. Consequence: the
D-11.1-01 skip is an off-hours optimisation; every business-hours run pays 54–83 min of full
validation, so SC-1 (< 75 min) cannot be met on business-hours runs by this design.
